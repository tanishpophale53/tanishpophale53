## About

I am a **Java Backend Engineer and product-focused software developer** building reliable systems across **payments, messaging, distributed workflows, observability, AI-powered analytics, and full-stack products**.

My core engineering work revolves around **Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, Docker, AWS, REST APIs, event-driven architecture, idempotency, webhooks, asynchronous processing, and production observability**.

Beyond traditional backend engineering, I build applied AI systems where **LLMs are treated as one component of a controlled software architecture rather than an unrestricted black box**. My work includes **RAG, schema-aware natural-language querying, AI analytics engines, vector databases, multilingual conversational interfaces, FastAPI services, and secure AI integrations**.

I enjoy solving the parts of software engineering that become important once systems meet the real world: **failures, retries, duplicate requests, latency, security, data consistency, scalability, monitoring, and maintainability**.

My product engineering mindset is simple:

> Build technology that solves a real problem, survives real traffic, and remains understandable when something goes wrong.
